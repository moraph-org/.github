# Security policy

This policy applies to every repository in the [moraph-org](https://github.com/moraph-org) organization on GitHub and to the services and apps built from them, including moraph.org. A repository without its own `SECURITY.md` uses this one.

## Supported versions

Only the latest release is supported: what is currently in production on the web, and the latest version published in the App Store or on Google Play. Older releases, preview deployments, branches and unreleased builds do not receive security fixes. Fixes ship as a new release.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not report a vulnerability in a public issue, pull request or discussion. Report it privately:

- Email **hello@moraph.org**, or follow [trust.moraph.com/vulnerability-reporting](https://trust.moraph.com/vulnerability-reporting). Both are the contacts published in [moraph.org/.well-known/security.txt](https://moraph.org/.well-known/security.txt).

Include what you found, where (product, URL, app version or repository), how to reproduce it, and the impact you expect. Please do not include more personal data than you need to show the problem.

## What to expect

We aim to acknowledge your report within 5 business days. We will keep you informed while we investigate and fix it, tell you when it is resolved, and credit you if you wish.

## Bug bounty

Moraph does not run a bug bounty program and does not pay for reports.

## Safe harbor

If you act in good faith under this policy (you avoid privacy violations, data loss and service disruption, access only the data needed to show the issue, do not exploit it further, and give us reasonable time to fix it before disclosing it), we will consider your research authorized and will not take legal action against you for it.
